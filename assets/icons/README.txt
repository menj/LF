Social icons
============

Monochrome 24x24 SVGs from "Minimalist Social & Platform Icons Pack" 2.8, released under the GNU
General Public License. Inlined by langgam_fikir_icon() (inc/social.php), recoloured with
currentColor, so a row of icons costs no extra requests.

LinkedIn and Scribd are deliberately NOT included: they come from Font Awesome Free (CC BY 4.0),
which requires a visible credit on the site. Brand names and logos remain trademarks of their
owners; use them only to link to official profiles or share buttons.
